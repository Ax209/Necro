Necromanic Panic

Tech Spec
    PC/Windows 
    Resolution = 128*128 (upscale to 512*512)

// Art Style    
    Low res pixel style, akin to Pico8.

// Game Story: 
    A tower defense where you play as a necromancer having to defend your magical crystal from tourists from Ghost Tours.

// Game Loop
    Defend from enemies walking along the path towards your Necro Crystal.

    Traverse the map and find graves to summon skellybobs. Choose the type to make them, and they will follow you around. 
    You can then tell them to stay put, follow you, or you can absorb them and take their powers.

    Earn Exp for completing levels (bonus for the first time), and then unlock new summons, upgrade your stats, and play 
    again for a better score.

// Upgradable stats
    Reduced Summon Cost
    Extend Summon Time
    Increase Total Skellybobs summoned
    Extend MP well
    Extent HP well
    

// Skellybobs
    Fighter
    Archer
    Stinger
    Defender (takes longer to kill, but does less damage)
    Pyrox (Casts fire spells which do damage over time)
    Cryox (Casts freeze spells, which can slow the enemy's attack and move)


// Loading Screen Hints
    "Summoning more skellybobs than your limit will cause the oldest to return to the earth."
